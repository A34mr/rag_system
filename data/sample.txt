Acme Corp Employee Handbook

Vacation Policy
All full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid vacation per month.
Vacation must be requested at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.
Unused vacation days carry over to the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days.

Password Reset
To reset your password, go to https://intranet.acme.local/reset
Enter your employee ID and answer your security question.
You will receive a reset link at your company email within 5 minutes.

Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval.

Refunds and Expenses
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of purchase.
Refunds for approved expenses are processed on the next payroll cycle.
